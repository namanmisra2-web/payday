FAQ

Questions we get a lot

Does checking my rate hurt my credit?

No. Checking your rate uses a soft inquiry, which is not visible to other lenders and doesn't affect your score. A hard inquiry only happens if you accept an offer.

Why do you need my bank details?

Your routing and account numbers let us deposit the funds directly and set up your scheduled payments. They're encrypted and never shared with third parties.

How fast will I get the money?

Most approved applicants are funded within one business day of accepting their offer.

Can I pay off my loan early?

Yes, any time, with no prepayment penalty. You only pay interest for the time you borrow.
